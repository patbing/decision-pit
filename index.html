Which AI keeps its cool in a market it has never seen?

  1. Pick players. Real decision models (live) or free rule-based bots (offline).
  2. Watch one seeded market. Every player sees the same prices and headlines. Confidence sets trade size; a second question scores their forecasts.
  3. Meddle. Fake rumors, market shocks and whispers show who is fooled. Then rematch the same seed.
